How We Approach the Work

  • • 1) Assess the damage and cause: We inspect the visible issue and check for moisture, movement, or repeat-failure risk.
  • • 2) Repair and prep properly: We patch, stabilize, and prep the area so the repair holds, not just covers the spot.
  • • 3) Match texture and finish: We blend texture and finish work so the repaired area fits the surrounding wall or ceiling.

What texture matching usually means

Older Tulsa homes often have popcorn, orange peel, or mixed textures from past work. We match as closely as the existing surface allows and set expectations when a perfect invisible blend is unlikely.
