Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Level & Anvil Solutions respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information You Provide

We may collect information that you choose to provide when you:

  • Call, text, or email us
  • Submit a website contact form
  • Request or schedule a consultation or service
  • Send project descriptions, addresses, photographs, files, or other information

This information may include your name, email address, telephone number, project address, preferred appointment times, project details, photographs, and messages.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information that is not necessary for us to evaluate or complete your project.

Website and Analytics Information

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use our website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the website
  • Approximate geographic area
  • Referring website or search source
  • Browser, device, and operating-system information
  • General interactions with website links and features
  • Internet Protocol address and cookie or device identifiers

Google may use cookies and similar technologies to collect and process this information. Analytics reports generally provide aggregated information and are not intended to identify you personally.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from websites that use its services by reviewing Google’s Privacy & Terms information for partner sites.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to questions and service requests
  • Evaluate, estimate, schedule, and perform projects
  • Communicate about appointments or ongoing work
  • Review photographs and project documentation
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Understand which website pages and services are useful to visitors
  • Maintain business and accounting records
  • Protect the website and prevent misuse
  • Meet legal, insurance, and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Information Sharing

We may share information when reasonably necessary with service providers that help us operate the business, such as:

  • Website hosting and technical-service providers
  • Google Analytics

  • Payment, accounting, insurance, or business-administration providers
  • Employees, subcontractors, consultants, or project professionals involved in evaluating or completing your work

These parties may process information only as necessary to provide their services or perform the requested work.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our legal rights, or to address suspected fraud, safety concerns, or misuse.

Project Photographs

Photographs or files you submit may be used to evaluate, estimate, document, or complete your project.

We will not intentionally publish photographs that clearly identify you, your child, your address, or sensitive personal belongings without permission. Project photographs may be used for business records, training, portfolios, or marketing when appropriate permission has been obtained or identifying details have been removed.

Cookies and Your Choices

Google Analytics may place cookies in your browser to distinguish visits and understand website usage.

You may limit cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website functions. Google also provides tools and settings that allow users to limit Google Analytics data collection.

You may contact us to:
