Scope

Soffit, fascia, siding, and trim

We repair and replace damaged exterior components when the condition of the home calls for it: loose siding courses, cracked or water-stained soffit material, fascia boards pulling away, and trim that no longer sheds water correctly.

Exterior painting should follow sound substrate work. If peeling paint points to moisture, we help map what to fix first so new paint does not seal in the wrong problem.

How We Evaluate Exterior Conditions

What we look at before recommending work

On site, we typically check:

  • • Soft, stained, or missing soffit and fascia material
  • • Loose, cracked, or water-marked siding and trim
  • • Paint failure that may be tied to moisture rather than age alone
  • • Splash-back, runoff, and downspout paths that keep wetting the same wall
  • • Whether a repair, a larger replacement, or a hold-and-monitor approach makes more sense
